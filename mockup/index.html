Factora — UI/UX design target

The ten screens from the Beginner Build Playbook (§2 UI/UX Blueprint, pages 4–10), extracted at original resolution into this folder. Build the UI to match these, not to a reinterpretation of them.

Design language (from the playbook)

  • Shell: dark navy sidebar, Factora logo/wordmark, plant selector, live-data indicator, search field, notifications, profile area.
  • Panels: clean enterprise dashboard — white/light content panels, blue accent, no random gradients, no excessive animation.
  • Machine states: healthy green, warning amber, critical red, maintenance blue/gray, offline gray — used consistently on every page and in the 3D twin.
  • Honesty: simulated telemetry is labelled as simulated; numbers come from the API, never invented in a component.
